# Code of Conduct

Sight Buddy is built for and with people who are blind or have low vision. We want everyone who takes part, whatever their background, ability or experience, to feel welcome and respected.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Be respectful and patient.** Assume good intent. Many contributors use screen readers; a slow reply or a terse message is often just the tools.
- **Describe accessibility problems plainly**, and listen when someone who lives with them tells you what works.
- **Give and accept constructive feedback** about the work, never about the person.
- **Unacceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes (including about disability), personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers:

- use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request, or
- open a private report through the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct:".

Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled in confidence. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
